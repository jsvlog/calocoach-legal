CaloCoach is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Calorie and nutrition targets are estimates based on the information you enter. AI meal analysis and coach replies are generated automatically and can be wrong about ingredients, portions, calories and nutrients.

Always consult a doctor or registered nutritionist-dietitian before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, managing a medical condition such as diabetes or an eating disorder, or taking medication. Never disregard professional advice because of something the app said.

CaloCoach has a free tier and one paid plan, CaloCoach Premium.

The free tier includes a daily limit on AI meal scans and AI coach messages; the app shows your remaining allowance. Everything else — calorie and macro tracking, water and weight logging, barcode lookup, trends and your health score — is free.

CaloCoach Premium removes those daily limits. It is sold through Google Play as an auto-renewing subscription: ₱199 per month or ₱1,499 per year. Payment is taken by Google Play, not by us — we never see or store your card details. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions; you keep Premium until the period you already paid for ends.

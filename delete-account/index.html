DELETION

Delete your CaloCoach data

Two ways: in the app, or by email.

Option 1 — in the app (instant)

Open CaloCoach and go to:

  • Daily tab → profile avatar → Settings → Delete All My Data

Confirm and everything is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — by email

Email lizardojohnsonc@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my CaloCoach data".

Because CaloCoach accounts are anonymous by default, include your account ID if you can find it (Settings → About shows it) so we can locate the exact account. If you linked an email, send the request from that address instead — that is enough. We action email requests within 7 days and confirm when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, age, gender, height, weight, goal weight, goal, activity level, dietary preference, health conditions you selected)
  • All meals, meal photos stored in our storage, and their nutritional analysis
  • Water logs, exercise logs, weight history and favourite foods
  • All AI coach messages and coach preferences/check-in settings
  • Daily AI usage counters and any content reports you submitted
  • The account itself (the anonymous user record, or the linked email)

If you have a Premium subscription

Deleting your CaloCoach data does not cancel your subscription. Google Play will keep charging you for an account that no longer exists.

Cancel first: open Google Play → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CaloCoach → Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the period you already paid for ends, then delete your data here.

What we keep

Nothing is kept after deletion, and there is no backup copy that outlives it. Deleting a single item (one meal, one water entry, one exercise log, one weight entry, one coach message) is also possible inside the app without deleting the account.

One exception: we do not hold card details or billing records at all. Your purchase history stays with Google Play and with RevenueCat, our subscription provider, for as long as their own policies require — we cannot delete those records on your behalf, but they contain no meal, photo or health data.

What deletion does not affect

Deleting your CaloCoach data does not uninstall the app or remove anything Google Play keeps about the install. You can uninstall the app afterwards in the usual way.
